1. Introduction and Commitment

David Nyanzwengye ("we", "us", "our", "the Developer") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Runyankore Smart Translator ("the Service", "the Application"). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Service, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

We respect your privacy rights and are committed to transparency about our data practices. This policy complies with applicable privacy laws, including the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, and where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Union.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when using the Service:

  • Translation Submissions: Words, phrases, and translations you submit for dictionary inclusion
  • Feedback and Communications: Name, email address, and messages when you submit feedback, bug reports, or suggestions
  • User-Generated Content: Any content you create or submit to the Service
  • Account Information: Currently, no account registration is required to use basic features

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you access the Service, we may automatically collect certain information:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type and version, screen resolution
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, features used, translation queries (anonymized)
  • IP Address: For analytics and security purposes (anonymized where possible)
  • PWA Installation Data: Whether the app is installed and usage patterns
  • Language Preferences: Preferred translation directions

2.3 Local Storage Data

The Service stores certain data locally on your device:

  • Translation History: Your past translations are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB or localStorage
  • Offline Dictionary: The dictionary database is cached locally for offline use
  • User Preferences: Your settings and preferences
  • PWA Assets: Service Worker caches app assets for offline functionality

Important: Translation history is stored ONLY on your device. We do NOT have access to your personal translation history unless you explicitly submit it.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes:

  • Provide and Maintain Service: To deliver translation services, process requests, and maintain functionality
  • Improve and Optimize: To analyze usage patterns and improve translation accuracy, user experience, and performance
  • Develop New Features: To understand user needs and develop new capabilities
  • Dictionary Enhancement: To review and incorporate user-submitted translations (with your consent)
  • Communication: To respond to your feedback, questions, and support requests
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security vulnerabilities
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms
  • Analytics: To understand how the Service is used and identify areas for improvement

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Compliance)

For users in the European Union, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Consent: When you provide feedback or submit translations, you consent to our processing of that information
  • Contractual Necessity: Processing necessary to provide the Service you request
  • Legitimate Interests: Processing for analytics, security, and service improvement where your rights do not override these interests
  • Legal Obligation: Processing necessary to comply with applicable laws

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following circumstances:

5.1 Service Providers

We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our Service ("Service Providers"), to provide the Service on our behalf, or to assist us in analyzing how our Service is used. These third parties have access to your information only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose. Current Service Providers include:

  • Hosting Providers: For server infrastructure and data storage
  • Analytics Services: For anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics, etc.)
  • CDN Services: For content delivery and performance optimization

5.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information where required to do so by law or subpoena, or if we believe that such action is necessary to:

  • Comply with a legal obligation
  • Protect and defend our rights or property
  • Prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with the Service
  • Protect the personal safety of users or the public
  • Protect against legal liability

5.3 Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred. We will provide notice before your information is transferred and becomes subject to a different Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls for any personal data we store
  • Regular Audits: Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure Development: Secure coding practices and regular updates
  • Data Minimization: We only collect information necessary for the Service

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You use the Service at your own risk.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy:

  • Local Storage Data: Remains on your device until you clear browser data or uninstall the PWA
  • Feedback Data: Retained for up to 2 years after resolution, unless you request deletion
  • Translation Submissions: Retained indefinitely as part of the dictionary (if accepted)
  • Analytics Data: Anonymized and aggregated, retained for up to 26 months

You can clear your local data at any time through your browser settings or by clearing the app data.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

8.1 Rights Under GDPR (EU Users)

  • Right to Access: Request copies of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request restriction of how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service
  • Right to Object: Object to our processing of your data
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time

8.2 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at dnyanzwengye@gmail.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 30 days. To verify your identity, we may require information to confirm your request.

8.3 Local Data Control

You can manage your locally stored data through your browser:

  • Clear Translation History: Use the "Clear History" button in the app
  • Clear All Data: Use browser settings to clear site data
  • Uninstall PWA: Remove the app from your device to delete all local data

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity and hold certain information. Cookies are files with a small amount of data that may include an anonymous unique identifier.

9.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the Service to function (e.g., for PWA installation detection)
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the Service

9.2 Managing Cookies

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our Service.

If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete such information from our servers immediately.

We take all steps reasonably necessary to ensure that your data is treated securely and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
